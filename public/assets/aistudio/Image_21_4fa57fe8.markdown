## Brand & Style

The design system projects unyielding reliability, calm transparency, and effortless precision for Indian car owners navigating vehicle maintenance. Car repair often triggers anxiety regarding hidden charges and spurious parts. The interface answers this with an uncompromising design ethos: pristine surfaces, rigorous typographic hierarchy, and unambiguous status communication that embodies the core promise: "Locked price. Zero surprises."

The design style is a synthesis of **Modern High-Trust Corporate** and **Soft Neomorphic Minimalism**:
- **Pristine, Air-Filled Canvases:** Grounded in cool, soft off-white backgrounds with pure white floating containers that feel immaculate and organized.
- **Architectural Typography:** Authoritative, geometric sans-serif headings anchored by crystal-clear, neutral body typography for effortless scanning of dense estimate breakdowns and milestone timelines.
- **Reassuring Materiality:** Tactile, subtly elevated surfaces with ultra-diffuse ambient shadows rather than harsh divider lines, reinforcing a premium, stress-free workshop lounge experience.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative structure with functional reassurance:

- **Primary (`#0B1F3A` - Deep Sovereign Navy):** Delivers bedrock security, institutional trust, and high legibility across structural headers, primary actions, and primary line items.
- **Secondary (`#14B8A6` - Precision Teal):** The energetic driver of progress, used for locked-in commitments, interactive accents, active tabs, and primary conversion triggers.
- **Surface & Canvas (`#F7F8FA` Canvas / `#FFFFFF` Container):** Creates natural contrast without aggressive border divisions. Slate-tinted neutral scales (`#64748B` for secondary text, `#94A3B8` for subtle borders, `#0F172A` for ink body) ensure high contrast under varying Indian sunlight and mobile display conditions.
- **Semantic Feedback System:**
  - **Success / Verified (`#16A34A` Emerald):** Explicitly denotes "Locked Price", OEM verification, and completed checkouts. Pair with `#DCFCE7` tint.
  - **Warning / In-Progress (`#F59E0B` Amber):** Denotes active technician bay inspections, awaiting approvals, and diagnostic alerts. Pair with `#FEF3C7` tint.
  - **Critical / Alert (`#DC2626` Crimson):** Marks critical component wear, declined safety inspections, and payment timeouts. Pair with `#FEE2E2` tint.

## Typography

The type scale combines the warm, geometric stature of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for prominent headers with the rigorous, neutral utility of **Inter** for data tables, inspection line items, and system readouts.

- **Indian Rupee (₹) Display Convention:** The `₹` symbol should match the optical weight of the associated numerals, rendered in `Plus Jakarta Sans` with non-tabular kerning for price tags and tabular tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) inside estimate and invoice breakdowns.
- **Price Transparency Rule:** Slashed original prices or itemized lab charges use `body-sm` (`#94A3B8`, line-through), followed directly by the locked total in `headline-md` or `currency-display` in `#0B1F3A` or `#16A34A`.
- **Text Color Hierarchy:** Primary titles utilize `#0B1F3A`, structural body text utilizes `#334155`, and meta descriptions utilize `#64748B`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt architectural rhythm, calibrated for single-hand mobile ease-of-use and expansive desktop transparency dashboards.

- **Layout Structure:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column responsive fluid grid with maximum container width of `1280px`, centered with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Service breakdowns adopt a 7:5 split (7 columns for inspection visual feeds and line items; 5 columns for sticky locked-price checkout summaries).
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `gutter: 1.25rem` and `margin: 1.5rem`.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `gutter: 1rem` and `margin: 1rem`. Sticky bottom action bars are used for instantaneous price lock confirmation without layout shift.
- **Component Breathing Room:** Generous internal padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) inside cards prevents the dense service list from appearing overwhelming.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation is established strictly through soft, ambient, slate-tinted drop shadows over a cool-tinted off-white background (`#F7F8FA`). Hard borders are replaced with tonal boundaries and micro-shadows to preserve an approachable, premium aesthetic.

- **Surface Neutral (Ground Zero):** `#F7F8FA` — Canvas foundation for background pages and side gutters.
- **Elevation Tier 1 (Base Cards, Form Fields, Tiles):** `#FFFFFF` surface with shadow `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.02)`. Optional 1px border using `#E2E8F0`.
- **Elevation Tier 2 (Hovered Cards, Vehicle Selection, Price Locks):** `#FFFFFF` surface with shadow `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.06), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.03)`.
- **Elevation Tier 3 (Modals, Inspection Detail Sheets, Floating Bottom CTAs):** `#FFFFFF` surface with shadow `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a unified `16px` (`1rem`) geometry for all major content cards and structural surfaces, creating balanced, modern tactility without feeling toy-like.

- **Cards, Sheets & Panels:** Uniformly styled with `rounded-lg` (16px / 1rem) to create smooth, approachable edges for complex mechanical data.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Buttons, Tab Strips):** Standardized with 8px to 12px radii for structural precision.
- **Badges, Tags & Milestones:** Full circular encapsulation (`rounded-full` / pill form) to distinguish state indicators clearly from structural content cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Navy (`#0B1F3A`) fill, crisp `#FFFFFF` text, 12px border radius, 48px standard touch height (54px on mobile booking actions). Micro-hover state shifts to `#132B4F`. Active state features a 0.98 scale transform.
- **Secondary / Accent:** Teal (`#14B8A6`) fill with `#FFFFFF` text, reserved for "Lock Price", "Approve Repair", and "Book Slot".
- **Ghost / Outline:** Transparent fill, `#0B1F3A` text with a 1px border in `#CBD5E1`. Hover initiates a `#F1F5F9` background fill.

### Status Badges & Chips
- Fully rounded pills (`border-radius: 9999px`) with 12px horizontal padding and 4px vertical padding.
- **Verified / Approved:** `#DCFCE7` background, `#15803D` text, paired with a solid 6px emerald status dot or thin shield icon.
- **In-Progress / Pending:** `#FEF3C7` background, `#B45309` text, accompanied by an animated pulsing amber indicator.
- **Declined / Attention Required:** `#FEE2E2` background, `#B91C1C` text.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- Pure `#FFFFFF` background set against `#F7F8FA`.
- 16px corner radius, `space-lg` (24px) internal padding, subtle 1px border in `#E2E8F0`.
- Includes a dedicated header section showcasing item name, OEM warranty label, and the locked ₹ price pinned cleanly to the top right.

### Input Fields
- White background with 1px border in `#CBD5E1`, 12px border radius, 48px height.
- Focus state activates a 1px border in `#14B8A6` along with a soft 3px halo `rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.15)`.
- Input fields for vehicle registration format license plates cleanly into uppercase tracked blocks (e.g., `MH 02 DX 4821`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- 20px box dimensions with 6px border radius for checkboxes; circular with inner dot for radios.
- Unchecked: 1.5px border in `#94A3B8`.
- Checked: `#0B1F3A` solid background with a white feather-line checkmark.

### Specialized Component: "Locked Price Guarantee" Meter
- A card featuring a solid `#14B8A6` left boundary accent, a clear lock glyph, text reading "Final Locked Estimate", and the total amount formatted as `₹ XX,XXX` with zero variable disclaimers.
